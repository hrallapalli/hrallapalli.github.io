I am currently a postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Section on Plasticity and Imaging of the Nervous System at the NINDS/NIH, working with Dr. Alan Koretsky. I am the recipient of an NIBIB/NIH K99/R00 Pathway to Independence Award, have filed a patent application on the technology developed from my current research, and have mentored nine students. I received my Ph.D. in Biomedical Science from New York University with Dr. Daniel Turnbull, and my B.S. in Biomedical Engineering from the University of California, Davis. I began my research career as a student in Dr. Sam Gambhir's laboratory at Stanford University.

I have directly supervised two post-baccalaureate, five undergraduate, and two high school students, most entering a laboratory for the first time. One of my post-baccalaureate mentees is second author on my 2024 Magnetic Resonance in Medicine paper and is now doing her Ph.D. in Neuroscience at Emory University. I serve on the NINDS Fellows Advisory Committee on Training, and I wrote the curriculum for the &ldquo;No Programming Required Image Processing&rdquo; workshop I founded.

I am funded by an NIH Pathway to Independence Award (K99/R00). I received the International Society for Magnetic Resonance in Medicine (ISMRM)&rsquo;s Magna cum laude merit award for an abstract describing early MAGIC results presented at the 2023 annual meeting. The World Molecular Imaging Congress 2025 program committee selected my abstract demonstrating MAGIC&rsquo;s long-range neural tracing and translational potential as among the most innovative at that meeting.
