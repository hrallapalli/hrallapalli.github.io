I'm a biomedical engineer and postdoctoral fellow at the National Institutes of Health, working with Dr. Alan Koretsky in the Laboratory of Functional and Molecular Imaging at NINDS. My work is supported by an NIBIB K99/R00 Pathway to Independence Award.

I did my PhD with Dr. Daniel Turnbull at New York University. Before that, I worked in Dr. Sam Gambhir's lab at Stanford and earned my BS in Biomedical Engineering at UC Davis. I founded and teach the "No Programming Required" image-processing workshop, and I mentor students through NIH postbac, summer-internship, and outreach programs.
